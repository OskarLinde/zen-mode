Within easier reach.

High positions move down with a quiet surface beyond the real board edge. Real corners stay intact, with at least six intersections across. All loaded variations and labels stay in view.

Open ••• → Outside board to compare Woven, Natural tatami, and Solid. Auto pairs the surround with your board appearance. Small (⅓-grid) margin is the default; choose None to remove the narrow mat border, and Bottom bar → Frosted glass or Solid. The glass reveals the real board or mat underneath.

Try transitions in the direct puzzle demo: ••• → Board transition → Pan & crossfade: stones sweep away as the camera moves, then it pauses 50ms before building the new position in random, alternating-color order. Site theme, Small margin and Pan & crossfade are the defaults for unset preferences. Existing choices stay saved; Steady, Reduce Motion and unsupported browsers skip the animation.
